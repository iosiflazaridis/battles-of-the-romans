# Where the Romans Fought

An interactive map, the Roman win-draw-loss record and the full list of the 1,709 named land and sea battles fought by the Romans,
from the wars of Romulus (753 BCE by the traditional date) to the fall of Theodoro at Mangup (1475): the Kingdom, the Republic, the Empire
(West and East as one state) and, after 1204, its successor states. Each battle records who fought in the Roman army, the reasons for any
doubt, and its sources.

**Open the page:** https://iosiflazaridis.github.io/battles-of-the-romans/

- `index.html`: the page (self-contained; the data are embedded)
- `img/`: the 18 images in full size (3200 × 1800); `thumb/`: their thumbnails
- `dl/`: the data as CSV, JSON and an Excel workbook (with the record tables and the entries left out, with reasons)

Sources: the ancient, medieval and modern historians, cited battle by battle in the data. Map: Natural Earth.
